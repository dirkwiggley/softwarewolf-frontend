Title: Knob End
Tagline: Elicyian Port
---
 A very shady port. 


**Town Notables and Locations**

- Mayor Benjamin Dover
- Hooker Bass - Harbormaster
- Guy Manley - Captain of the guard.
- Liddy Gates - Magestrate (Judge)
- Barbara Dwyer - General Store owner. A bit prickley
- Frank Sanbeens - owner of The Nobody Inn
- Dusty Broom - employee of The Nobody Inn
- Sue Fley - cook at The Nobody Inn 
- Justin Case - Blacksmith/gunsmith
- Rhoda Book - Author. Currently working on a second volume of Orcs of the Western Sea
- Paige Turner - Rhoda's sister. Also an accomplished author of works of fiction
- Les Newcomb - owner of Western Exotics, a small import/export business. Donated the property in the center of town that was made into Knob Park
- Miss Nancy Moss - owner of Nancy's Harbor Cafe
- Jack Hammer - Bartender at Nancy's Harbor Cafe
- Jack Potts - Gambler frequently at Nancy's Harbor Cafe
- Melba Crisp - Clothier
