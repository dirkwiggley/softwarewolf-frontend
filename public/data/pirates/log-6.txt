Title: Here we go!
Tagline: Session 6
---
The Legend of Hamalot - By Page Turner

Voyage for our dashing damsel aboard the black ship Wicked Heart amongst the motley crew of pernicious pirates was one fraught with danger and adventure. Furthermore Morgan’s delicate constitution required frequent stops along the perilous coastline which brings us to the fateful red dawn morning where that monstrous ship entered into the emerald green bay of Knob’s End.

The Lady, for that is how she presented herself even amongst her surly sailor companions, came to visit our humble abode with a curious young lad in tow. My sister Rhoda and I, having made her august acquaintance on a previous occasion sought to hear the latest tall tales concerning her action filled adventures amongst the demons of the deep and monsters of the mainland. On this particular occasion the Lady of Legend did not disappoint. That is when inspiration struck me once again and I began to plot out my latest fictional escapade concerning my adventuring female protagonist who dared the deep, but this time alongside her trusted companion and perhaps...lover?

Sure I had a vision for who would feature on the cover as my protagonist, but who could be the handsome hero in order to sell a thousand copies! In a euphoric epiphany I knew who the dashing dilettante must be! The Lady Morgan travelled with one such individual whose vibrant visage could make a married woman weak in the knees. That Boreland braggadocio Barend’s image would be a perfect counterpoint to mine on the cover. Swoon! The Lady Morgan kindly acquiesced to facilitate the proposal.

The coveted compact agreed, the Lady then ventured out on a more mundane mission to acquire ingredients for some hellish howitzer of sorts for use aboard that black beast in the bay. Her commission complete the lady once again departed with her cantankerous crew and sailed out on white capped waves.

The next night was dark and dreary and the callow crew wary of the denizens of the deep kept careful watch over the moonlit waves. One observant lookout spotted another solemn ship on the ocean’s horizon backlit by the harvest moon. It was the Lutefisk, a villain-filled vessel that had upon prior occasions attempted foul play upon the Lady Morgan and crew of the Wicked Heart. However on this particular night, naught but death and deviltry filled the air. As the Wicked Heart drew close the wail of spectral sailors could be heard emanating from the Lutefisk’s hull. Not taking any chances the Lady figured it was a fine time to test the magnificent munitions she had created. Two thunderbolts lanced forth from the coppered canons of the Wicked Heart piercing the haunted hull of the ghost ship and detonating the vile vessel in a fireball of garish green that could be seen for miles.

With the Lutefisk resting in Davey Jones’s ocean crypt, the curmudgeonly crew was once again able to sleep a bit easier knowing that disaster had been averted by the quick actions of the Magnificent Morgan.

Days later as a delicious dawn broke over the hazy horizon, a mysterious island was spotted. In desperate need of supplies the hardy heroes ventured forth in search of supplies and secrets. It was then that they stumbled across the Legendary Lost Temple of Artur, protector of the Lands! As our wary wanderers stepped upon the sacred stones adorning the ruined reliquary the sequestered spirits of Artur, Gwenyth and Lancil possessed the Lady and members of her courageous coterie.

Possessed by ancient animus, they reenacted the betrayal of Artur by his wanton wife Gwenyth and former stouthearted steward Lancil. Shots were fired and in the end Artur sought solace in the bosom of his salacious sister Morgan (no relation...right?).
