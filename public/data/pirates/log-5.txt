We left our heroes fighting for their very lives amidst the Savage Badokadonk Orc War Camp in their attempt to steal the Flesh of St. Bias to fulfill their Geas to Billy Joe McAllister.  However the War Chief Burnyoazz and his pet Shaman Chargrilled had other ideas. Inside the camp, the infiltration team consisted of Jonathon and Nicholas, who actually got his hands on the Ambergris.  Everyone else was outside the compound looking to provide cover fire for the retreating heroes. Alas Jonathon’s attempt to free the orc slaves resulted in their turning on their rescuer and dragging him back into their slave pen to administer a frightful beating.

 

All around the cursed camp, Trees animated to life to attack the heroes using their branches in a attempt to draw them into cavernous maws that split open from the trunk..  In addition to the threats to the team outside, war horns sounded the arrival of Badokadonk skirmishers that were returning to run down those on the outside astride their Razorbacks.  

 

Nicloas found the Ambergris to be anathema to him, and if whathisname (Eric Z’s character) had arrived only minutes earlier from the town with said message he would have been better prepared.  Holding the vile vomit caused a rapid deterioration of the health of our aquatic adventurer.

 

Sensing Jonathon’s plight, Morgan dropped a molotov cocktail into the maw of the tree bent on snacking on her and sent ‘Lucky’ off to see what help he could provide.  “Lucky” gave her that, are you kidding me look, but flew off in the general direction of the camp. As she turned around to offer aid to the others a Savage orc rode her down and lanced her through the chest.  

 

The others were making valiant efforts in their fights with the outriders, but the Badokadonks from inside the camp were marshalling out now that the slave revolt had been quelled.

 

Nic found himself swallowed by one of the Cursed trees, only to miraculously emerge from it sans the Ambergris.  Free of the burden he was able to fully engage both the Warchief and Shaman leader along with Hellcat, or Smythe - one of those two I think.  

 

Barend was his usual rampaging self and hewed down numerous Orcs and eventually acquired a Razorback mount of his own..  Not to be outdone, Porter summoned a massive hurricane force windstorm to smash dozens of orcs into the fortifications.

 

Mad woke up to a stormy shore with five silvery specters of sailors milling about.  One approached her and informed her that he was trading his life for hers per his pact with Davey Jones.  Eager at a chance to get back at the bastards that ruined her blouse, Morgan jumped at the chance and appeared atop the gatehouse blasting a Shaman off the structure with a well placed Hell Fire round.  

 

Soon the Savage Orcs were defeated, once their Shaman Chargrilled went down the curse that was powering the rest of the Badokadonk Orcs was dispelled and the remainder fled off into the night.  The battle won, the crew found out that Lucky had convinced the Orc slaves to take Jonathon back the Wicked Heart. Morgan found the body of the sailor that took her place and gave him a proper burial promising to send word to his family as to his final fate.

 

Once again aboard the Wicked Heart the crew set out to return to McAllister to deliver the Ambergris.  Upon the sea they run into Scaredy Cat and his Bathysphere while evading marauding Castellians flotillas.  

 

Eventually the Wicked Heart arrived at the shoals neighboring Davey’s underwater grotto.  The Heroes proceeded underwater thanks to some spellcasting and met with McAllister once again, delivering the Ambergris to him.  In return as reward he provided the crew with a key, inkwell and map to some uncharted islands where they could find treasure. However there is some bad news, Billy Joe informs the crew that his master has been taken by the Mages of Legend from the sealed realm of iforgotitsname.

 

The Crew took the Wicked heart to an area of the ocean where there were supposed to be no islands, and yet with the map they found an archipelago.  The second Island was strange as there had previously been structures upon it, but there were no two stones connected as all was in ruin. However after some investigation the crew found a door and used the key given to them by Billy Joe to open it into another space.  Inside was a room with a large drafting table and a weary looking dwarf. the dwarf, Ian provided the crew with a log book and instructions that everyone needed to write their names in the book if they were to take the prized dwarven Cog that was to be their reward. The crew agreed, thanked the dwarf and upon exiting back outside witnessed a Cog as black as night arise out of the surrounding bay.   The Crew took possession of the Cog which was odd in many ways. However as the first raiding Castellians discovered, the Cog was not friendly to border actions from those whose names were not written in the ship’s book. The very decks opened beneath the borders and they were swallowed screaming as bristling teeth like timbers ground them to bits.

 

Some of the crew shuddered at this turn of events, but were thankful/  Eyes in the hull, bleeding from the floorboards, tentacles devouring sea life under the ship.  Only some of the mysteries that the crew discovered about their newest vessel. A new scourge of the sea had set sail, destination...next time!
