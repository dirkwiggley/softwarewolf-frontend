The crew returns to the Wicked heart and attempts to leave the accursed isle of the Addrakarians while Abominations attempt to board the ship.  The Wicked Heart had come into the shallows, so it gave numerous abominations plenty of time to attempt to board the ship, only to be repelled by the heroic efforts of the topside crew of Mad, Barred and the doctor.

However entering the deeper waters all was not safe, as a lone Addrakarian frigate came around the isthmus and attacked.  The Wicked heart made short work of the vessel, however it’s illithid captain boarded the ship and after a short skirmish disappeared.  However it was soon discovered that he had made off with the psionically stupored Jonathan.  

The captain’s chest with log book was uncovered from the wreckage and the Baroness coerced Lucky to read it to uncover what the captain’s interest in Jonathan may be.  She missed his excellent breakfast beignets after all.  She was able to decipher a pictogram that revealed Jonathan would be used as a battery to open numerous gates for the addrakarian fleet to enter our seas via the lighthouse.

The team set out to sail to the known whereabouts of the lighthouse in an attempt to get there before the Illithid could bring Jonathan.

Interlude involved the Doctor reliving past nightmares and the Baroness sunning herself on the deck watching the Captain perform acrobatic feats of dexterity amidst the rigging.  

Oh yeah, Morgan uncovered the fact that Lucky was a transformed Addrakarian wizard sent to spy on us and report back to his masters.  This is a joke right?  Being asked to read Addrakarian text woke up something primal in lucky that reverted a part of his personality back into mad wizard mode.  However he promised to eat Morgan only after she was dead, so there was that.

While waiting for the lighthouse to appear in the designated waters, two Castellan ships were spotted approaching the Wicked Heart.  During the day we scared them off, but at night the ship stealthily crept up on the two ships as the winds had been calmed by Porter.  The Warship was no match for the surprise broadside ram of the Heart and she quickly capsized and sank to the bottom.  
After one large tentacle cleared the marines off the smaller sloop, their captain saw reason and struck colors.  The two ships were the SeaShells and Sea Shore.  They were in the area to meet with another ship, but we can’t remember the name. 

Apparently they had a prisoner onboard the Seashells which was now nestled at the bottom of the ocean.  Good thing we sent our man from Germantis down to rescue Jonathan and bring him back onboard.  

Now all we had to do was wait for the other ship to show up and find out who is behind all this.

End Session One

Epilogue - 

Morgan felt really really bad that she broke Lucky and with Jonathan back knew how important his fine feathered friend was.  So she consulted with the mages on board and devised a plan to give lucky the essence of Davey Jones that she had acquired in the hopes that Davey Jones spirit would revert Lucky back to his old friendly self.  Yes this would be the same persona that was spying on us the whole time for the enemy.  Who knew?  

Before the ritual, Lucky gave Morgan his journal - just in case.  Perhaps if she had the time to read it, or understood Addrakarian she would have learned that it was a full correspondence of all out activities from Lucky and orders from his Illithid Masters.

Details.

The spell goes off with Lucky participating in the ritual as well.  Hey look Lucky is also a Mage.  Wasn’t there something about three mages working magic together?  If only a mage playing PLAYER was present to warn the rest of us.  End result, Lucky took in a portion of Davey Jones’s power and knowledge and with it tore down the Curse that prevented easy transport from the Addrakar homeworld and our own.  

Upside, mages didn’t have to worry about that silly curse anymore.

Why is the Wicked Heart rocking violently?  We wonder from the safety of the ritual room below decks.  Going topside we see a leviathan serpent cresting out of the water with a large brass palace on it’s neck region the size of the Wicked Heart.  Many Illithids can be seen staring at us from the openings.
