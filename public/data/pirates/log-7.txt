During the Addrakonian inspired zombie outbreak in Strathmore, Jonathan set about with Dr. Smythe to divulge a cure for the Zombie Plague that is gripping the city.  

Lady Morgan head to Rhoda Book’s House where Paige had also took up residence and instructed them and their servants to board up the house and prepare for a siege.  Zombie were wandering the streets and busting into houses everywhere.  

As Morgan is boarding up the home two zombies break in - Morgan shoots and kills both.  She did not know that they could be cured at this point.  She then retires to the upper room overlooking the manor house grounds armed with her Zombie killing blunderbuss.

Meanwhile Captain Barend organizes a crew to make a run at the courthouse to recover our ‘fines.’  He disperses (temporarily) a group of ruffians who are looking to take advantage of the situation in the same district as Rhoda Book’s home.  They then pick up Morgan and the group heads to the Courthouse.  Breaking into the courthouse Nico runs off the ruffians who had followed us.  We recover the funds, plus a little extra.  However in breaking into the courthouse we made a lot of noise and attracted a lot of the infected who were looking for a fight.

Morgan notices that there are men infected, women infected and little kid infected.  This culls her bloodlust and looks for an alternate exit.  Spotting an office to the side with an exterior window, she lays down a smoke grenade and makes for the room.  However as the party is trying to extract themselves an Addrakarian in Royal Guard guard garb teleports in and looks surprised that the party is her and throws some SPy…ders around.  These attach to Hellcat and Nico (See the shadow things from Babylon 5).   Barend notices these shadow parasites and removes them from the infected.  They immediately collapse.  We take them to Rhoda’s and she is able to provide smelling salts and some medical care.  They awake.

We make our way back to the ship.  Morgan prepares the ships cannon to fire an aerosol cure into the air.  Johnathon does a stellar job of mass producing the cure, Mad does a great job assembling the shot to be fired and Hellcat does an exemplary job of firing the shot into the air above the city.

Captain Barend should not be allowed my cannon.

Eventually we organize the cities defenses to spread the cure.

We then ransack Baron Martin Shekel’s room and Nico discovers secret papers that are orders and various other information.  

We work with the bureaucracy, getting the governor involved who is very grateful for how we saved his city.  He grants us all kinds of gifts and allows us to ignore the Baron’s patents.

The plot from the Baron was to become a patent troll to gather monies to support their hidden base.  The Zombie plague is a mystical adjunct to the Malaise.

The Addrakarians - are looking to capture the abomination (Jonathan) and take him to a secret base for experimentation.

Chief Doctor of Strathmore - Doctor Duncan Forsythe.

We are invited by the Governor, to a banquet in our honour.

We get land grants.

Barend is made a Captain of the Ellycian Navy.

A Pardon for Hellcat - from Ellycia.

We depart.  

A week out from the hidden Addrakarian base, we encounter three fleets.  2 groups of Addrakarian ships 3 and 2.  And one group sent to help us that includes a ship flying the colors of the Secret Book Club.  A blue book on a yellow field.

The Wicked Heart has 6 guns to a side for a full broadside.

We sink three of the ships.

The other two flee.

Morgan acquires pieces of the hull for examination and study to create effective hellfire countermeasures

We then sortie forth to the secret Island base

We travel up the river.

We enter the Addrakarian Temple.

Davey Jones is chained to the far wall.

An Elder Brain is suspended in the ceiling.

There are 6 addrakarians that interpose themselves and attack us.  We are in distorted space.

The Elder Brain is killed.

The remaining Addrakarian guards are killed by a psychic blast when the Elder Brain dies (To Nico’s tremendous power).

Davey Jones turned into a small blue ball which Morgan picked up.
