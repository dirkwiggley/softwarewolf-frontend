Title: Two Barrow Down
Tagline: Elicyian Port
---
 A very shady port. 


**Town Notables and Locations**

- The Nobody Inn – Frank Sanbeens owner of the Nobody Inn.   
- Dusty Broom – odd job guy at Nobody Inn. 
- Sue Fley – Cook at Nobody Inn.
- Miss Betsy Moss - owner of Betsy's Harbor Cafe
- Jack Knife - Bartender at Betsy's Harbor Cafe
- Jack Potts - gambler
- Liddy Gates – Magistrate (Judge) 
- Les Newcomb – owner of small import/export business dealing in exotics. (Mage)
- Cody Fendant – thief – will try to hire the boat to take a bunch of goods to market elsewhere. 
- Hooker Crooke – another thief 
- Scar deKatt - Mage. Actually not *in* town but somewhat near it. Operates a bath-o-sphere and travels in secret under the waves (mostly).
