**Avaritia**
A merchants guild stretching back before the founding of most of worlds countries. Controlled by a few families spread across the known world the nature of the guild has changed over time. After taking control of different markets, shipping companies, or trade routes, disasters have set back the plans of the Avaritia on several occasions. They now prefer not to control things but rather to take advantage of knowledge and timing to continue in profitable though short term operations. In recent generations they have also placed greater emphasis on smuggling and other illegal activities. The head of the guild is in Paese Del Vino.

**Navigator's Guild**

The Navigator's Guild is an ancient, worldwide organization. They have offices in every major (and some minor) ports and the guild is apolitical. The guild sells maps and general weather predictions when available. Additionally, anyone may join the guild for an annual fee. Members are allowed to purchase services at a discount. Members may apply to become a sanctioned navigator. A sanctioned navigator can command twice the salary (or more) of an ordinary ships navigator. The test to become a sanctioned navigator however is very difficult. It includes:

- *Possessing a list of good references*
- *Knowing at least three languages*
- *Being able to recreate a map perfectly after a brief study*
- *Knowing the mapping styles of at least three countries (and changes to those styles over time)*
- *Being able to name many locations from poorly drawn maps*
- *Knowing a long list of tidal information from various locations*
- *Being able to estimate the travel time from a given port to it's destination given, date of departure, ship type, a shipping manifest, and a crew manifest*
- *Possessing knowledge of the effects of rough seas/weather on ships*
- *Knowing a list of major currents*
- *Knowledge of local nautical customs*

<br />

The guild takes it's position in the world very seriously. It does not permit members or sanctioned navigators to trade information that it has sold to them. Nor does it allow the free trade of maps and navigational equipment. It's reputation is paramount and any members found to sully the good name of the guild is driven out and blacklisted, preventing them from finding reputable employment.


They have influence with harbormasters, merchants, and ship captains worldwide.


The guild library is world famous and probably the most complete history of the world is available there.


At present the Master Navigator (current head of the guild) is **Vidente Ciego**, a Casteelian who is wanted by the Church for heresy. Needless to say, he is no longer a citizen of Casteel. He is notable for being unhampered by his blindness.
