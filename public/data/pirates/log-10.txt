Title: We're gonna need a bigger boat
Tagline: Session 10
---
And of course I had lots of fun importing Felix for a game of Pirates to give me a chance to allow my sense of Jonathon to stabilize after his alien experiences. It was exciting to see how many ways good Capt. Berend would manage to cheat the poor lad. Even I was caught off guard by 5 gold sounding like a reasonable share to him after I brought the gig which included the 500gp bounty for the Borelandean Captain Dutch Ovan of the good ship, Heyoo Smeltit Deltit (nobody gets away!), plus the contents of that ship's treasure chest AND the value of the captured, slightly damaged, good ship itself. Sure, Felix is terrible at math...but even he can figure this doesn't add up.

But then, rather then give a fair share as promised for the subsequent foray against the Elysian merchant ship, the Punchbowl, which the lad determined was repainted as a different Elusian merchant ship, the Afterberth, which had been captured by Castelleans 6 months ago and was now manned entirely by a Castellean Spy Crew in League with the wicked mage, Morgoth and his tricksy, huge spider to raise a hideous, living Warship, The Bellicose, from the corrupted, malformed bodies of the stolen wretches of society...hunhh~hunhhh. Wait, where was I? Oh yeah, rather than give the poor kid a fair share for all that, Berend obviously gave the high-sign for Mad Morgan to use too much powder for the torpedo to blow the Afterberth to kindling, sinking whatever treasure and crew to the bottom of the Strathmore harbor! Subsequent to that catastrophe, if there had been anything on board the monstrous Bellicose, borne of corpses and aberrational magicks, that was worth carrying out which might've meant something as a 1/4 share...we'll sure never know!!

So poor Felix came out of that whole adventure covered in the blood of a big, ugly jump-spider and one of the evil, living ship's horrifically beating hearts, pretty banged up, disrespected (he's kind of sure...though there was also a strange confidence granted to him), terrified beyond reason by the grotesque plot beats of the story (and yeah, this is the same kid who saw those cruel processes for making chocolate back home), and with only a measly four gold pieces in his pockets because he had to use one to gain some of above the information!!

Thanks a lot, Rob!

He is returning back to Everward, disillusioned but at least certain he's solved the Mystery of the Missing Old Drunk Guys for the Boss back home. Maybe his travelling companion from Everward, Victor, the apprentice mage who abandoned him to go on to Nob End for some unspoken reason...will catch up with him on the road. 

Jonathon Morstyn will return in the next Pirate Adventure.
