Title: Port Gullsaim
Tagline: Elicyian Port
---
 Port Gullsaim (Kingdom of Elicya)  Known for its overabundance of sea gulls and for being an out-of-the-way harbor. Though the designation of harbor is generous.  

<br />

**Town Notables**

- Governor Joaquin Tuoabar
- Captain Conrad Steadman
- Captain of City Guard
- Harbormaster Roscoe Portly
- Tom and Jerry – Bully stevedores
- Shipwright Leakey Windsor
- Miller William Wheaton
- Clever Broker – Trader 3rd class working for the Bhrat Trading Company.
- The towns master merchant.  Durgan Broker – Clever's son. Fish merchant and naturalist.
- Squint – old man weather predictor, watcher over fishers. He knows how to magnetize iron or steel and makes simple compasses for the fisher fleet.
- Lobster Johnson – head of the Johnson family. The catch lobsters and crabs.
- Rusty Naesymth - blacksmith
- Diedre Desire – Owner of the Awl Inn. She was from a leather working family and her (deceased) husband used the dowry to open the Awl Inn. She also sells masterwork leather work goods when she gets time to make them.
- Doctor Robin Leach
- Captain Silver Flask – a nominally successful pirate captain of the Furious Beaver who frequented Port Gullsaim before his recent demise.

<br />

**Events**

There is an annual fishing rally that occurs on the unluckiest day of the year for the town. Everyone who can participates in the rally or in the various rescue efforts as this is considered an event that lessens the bad luck for the town for the rest of the year. The winner is the person who brings back the largest/heaviest whole fish before sunset. It is typical for no one to win the competition and the entrance fees roll over to the next year.
 
**Characters who grew up in Port Gullsaim get +1 on Throwing skill and the Dodge Edge.**
