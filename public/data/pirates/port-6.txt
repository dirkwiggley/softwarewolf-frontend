Title: Tarberry 
Tagline: Elicyian Port
---
Nestled between territories claimed by the kingdoms of Agrillian and Elicya, it was a small farming settlement notable for it's sugar plantation and rum distillery. The distillery and plantation burned down a decade ago and nearly all trading ships have stopped sailing there. Most of the towns skilled laborers left with the last trading ships and life has settled into a slow pace. There are still trades (miller, cooper, cloth maker, tanner) but none of note. There is a blacksmith but his forge is usually cold for lack of iron to work.

Still there are several small farms producing various foods, cotton, and hemp. They provide the materials for a small number of tradesmen in town to keep a local economy afloat. The town also has several hunters (mostly boar and smaller game) and fishers but there is no shipwright to build anything larger than a skiff so few locals venture far beyond the town. 

There are occasional incursions by small orc raiding parties but they are few and far between and the local hunters are always vigilant for signs that any orcs might be near. Most of the towns inhabitants are moderately well armed and willing to fight, so orc incursions are rare.

**Notable Locations**

- The Alligator Spear Tavern – Probably the largest building still standing in town, the Alligator Spear Tavern used to sell locally produced rum and beer in addition to meals of local fish and game. Though the tavern no longer sells rum since the destruction of the distillery. The tavern is named after a stone artifact found on the Kayllin sugar plantation, a 1' long stone cylinder with an 8” diameter. One end has a symbol of an alligator pierced by a spear and the other end looks like an over sized hand hold. The 'alligator rock' as it's known went missing and was presumed stolen for about two weeks and was mysteriously returned shortly after the sugar plantation and rum distillery burned down. 
- Kayllin sugar plantation – Some of the surrounding swamp land was drained to make the plantation, which produced several successive bumper crops of sugar. Run the by prestigious Kayllin family, the plantation provided sugar used in their rum distillery for which the town became briefly famous before both the plantation and distillery burned to the ground. Most of the family perished in the fires but Gordon Kayllin, the patriarch of the Kayllin family survived and refused to rebuild. He became a recluse, living in a small hut on the plantation grounds feeding, reading, and singing to the alligators that moved in as the swamp reclaimed the land. A group of townsmen went to the farm to get old man Kayllin to turn over his property so that they could rebuild the plantation and distillery but none of them returned to town and when a second group came to see Kayllin to ask about it, he asked them to please not try to retake the plantation and let the swamp take it back. It was obvious at that point he had clearly lost his mind and the townsmen left him alone. 
- Doc Robert Edwards Junior – Doctor of dentistry and surgery. Son of previous physician Doc Edwards. His wife Persephonie Edwards make poultices and unguents used to treat fevers and various ailments. 
- Kyle Smyth – Blacksmith. The shortage of iron has led Kyle to work with Pershphone Edwards to develop a local resin that hardens bone and wood to near iron like hardness. He sells various tools and other items made with 'amber fastness' far more often than metal. He occasionally partners with Kora Lamplight who is the local tanner, to work on items of mixed construction. 
- Evin and Eowyn Evinrude and family – The largest fishing family in town. Both Ervin and his wife Eowyn (the patriarch and matriarch of the family) are well know as the best story tellers in town as well as repository of the towns history. The family is renown for their ability to predict the weather and skill in fishing.   
- Jess Windthorpe – Daughter of the last town mayor. While the town hasn't officially had a mayor since her father died, she is considered the de facto mayor and is called upon to settle the rare dispute. The mayors house is now used as a sewing business, employing several people involved in making anything out of cloth.   
- Unowned buildings in disrepair: Lighthouse Warehouse 
- Sshawn – leader of Lizard men. Has prepared a 'book' of the history of lizard men which he learned when the temple of Ssorhol was uncovered by Gordon Kaylin. 
- Loren – Grand daughter of Ervin and Eowyn Evinrude. She will become a water witch some day. Today though, she's a happy young girl. 
- Water Witch Isadore – Old swamp witch rumored to be living in the vicinity.
