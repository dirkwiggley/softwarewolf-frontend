Title: Pontena 
Tagline: Castellian Port
---
A large town with a strong fort and a substantial port. This fortified town is a regular stop of the Castillian treasure fleet.

<br />

**Notable People**

- Governor Estrega Alvar Macarena 
- Admiral Victor De los Reyes Ortega
- Cardinal Villano Mala Persona

**Characters from Pontena gain a free reroll on any failed persuasion or intimidation checks.**
