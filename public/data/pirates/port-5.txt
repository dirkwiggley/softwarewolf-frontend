Title: Creutzfeldt
Tagline: Elicyian Port
---
This is an island farm hold. Owned by the Rickets family, the farm produces high quality tobacco and the patriarch, Donald is a woodworker of some renown. Known for his inlaid, hand carved pipes and tobacco boxes. His wife, Regina, and daughters produce elegantly painted pottery.

The island has about sixty inhabitants, mostly farm workers though there is a small inn and trading post on the island by the port.

<br />

**Notable Locations**

- Hanson's Tavern is a small tavern catering to merchants who sail in for the local tobacco. It has a large patio with a covered roof. Hardy Hanson, the proprietor (and brother-in-law of Donald Rickets) has a clever system of panels that he can put up around the patio to accommodate larger numbers of overnight guests.
- Genny's General Store, is a shop with many miscellaneous and sundry items a traveler might find themselves in need of. The proprietress is Henna Hanson, the wife of Hardy Hanson. The Hanson's children are generally running around in the store.
- A four watch towers have been erected around the port and near the mansion.
